# Vibe feature ideas

## Prototype features already included
- Home feed with sample posts
- Create a text post
- Like and unlike posts
- Search the feed
- Light and dark theme
- Vibe World preview card

## Ideas for future versions
1. **Vibe World** — discover public interest spaces and community events.
2. **Vibe Communities** — groups built around hobbies, learning and local interests.
3. **Vibe Together** — shared activities such as study sessions or creative challenges.
4. **Vibe Map** — opt-in discovery of public events and community places, with privacy controls.
5. **Vibe Creator Tools** — simple tools for creators to plan and organize posts.
6. **Vibe Business Pages** — profiles for small businesses and community projects.

## Safety and privacy principles
- Make profiles private by default for younger users.
- Provide block, report and mute controls before public launch.
- Do not reveal a user's live location.
- Collect as little personal information as possible.
- Add human-reviewed community rules and moderation processes.
